Refund and Cancellation Policy

Below is information about Qbic Catalyst Partners Pvt. Ltd ("QBIC") refund and cancellation policy.

1. Direct Sales

1.1 Can I cancel my order?

  • Before Dispatch: You may cancel your order any time before it is dispatched. Once dispatched, the order cannot be cancelled.
  • Cancellation Fee: A cancellation fee of Rs 5,000/- may apply if the order is cancelled after the specified time window and such request and fee will be at the sole desertion of the facilitating partner.
  • Subscription Fee: Cancellation or refund are not applicable for Subscription Orders.

1.2 How do I exercise the Right to Cancel?

  • To cancel, inform us of your decision by contacting Customer Support Number mentioned on the website.

1.3 Cancellation by Retail Partner

  • In cases where the Retail Partner supplying an order cancels the order due to unforeseen circumstances, a full refund will be initiated for prepaid orders.

1.4 Effects of Cancellation, Refunds, and Returns

  • Refunds will be processed within 30 calendar days of your cancellation notification.
  • You must return products in their original condition within 14 days of cancellation.
  • A cancellation fee of Rs 5,000/- will be deducted from your refund.
  • All payments will be refunded only upon return and inspection of the purchased product.
  • Cancellation or refund are not applicable for Subscription Orders.

1.5 Payment Processing

  • If your order related to the product was processed through a third-party financial scheme (lender), refunds will be processed based on the terms and conditions outlined by the lender.
  • The refund process may take additional time for funds to reflect in your account depending on the lender's policy.
2. General Provisions

2.1 Delivery

  • Product Orders will be delivered by the Retail Partner or its designated party.

2.2 Invoice Generation

  • Invoice will be generated by the Retail Partner or its designated party.

2.3 Returns Policy

  • Applicable only for eligible products.
  • Returns are subject to the Retail Partner's specific return policy, which may vary by product category. The return policy displayed on the product page will prevail.
  • Conditions for returns include:
    • The product must be unused, undamaged, and in original packaging.
    • All accompanying accessories, manuals, and warranty cards must be returned.

2.4 Returns Pick-Up and Processing

  • During pick-up, the product will be checked for:
    • Correct product
    • Complete product (including accessories and manuals)
    • Unused condition
    • Undamaged packaging
  • If any conditions are not met, the field executive has the right to refuse the return.

2.5 General Rules for Returns

  • If a product accessory is missing or damaged, the Retail Partner may either replace the accessory or issue a credit, subject to the lender and brand policy and explicit consent of the brand.
  • Returns for damaged or defective products will be honoured only if reported within 24 hours of delivery.

2.6 Brand Warranty and Service

  • For products requiring installation, returns will only be accepted if installed by authorized personnel.
  • Warranty claims will be subject to the brand's terms and conditions.

2.7 Refund Processing

  • Not applicable for Subscription Orders
  • Refunds for cancelled orders will be processed within 30 days.
  • Refunds will be remitted only to the original payment account.

2.8 Disputes

  • Any disputes arising out of cancellations or returns will be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in Bangalore, India.

For further assistance, please contact Customer Support Number mentioned on our website.
